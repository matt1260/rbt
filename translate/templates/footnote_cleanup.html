Footnote clean-up

Puts New Testament footnotes in one consistent form: the same paragraphs, lists and headings, no stray classes, fonts or pasted page markup. The wording never changes. Every clean-up downloads a backup of what it replaced, and Undo or Restore puts it back. Numbering gives a book's footnotes whole numbers again, in reading order.
